                                                              Instalacion de Python en Windows 10 u 11

1.Ingrese a la web oficial de [Python](https://www.python.org)

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cea9df83-3149-4e43-bed0-ad5fc84b5776

2. Dirijáse a [Download;Windows](https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/)

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e4c7f5d-3354-4eaf-b71a-a19ab55603e5

3.Haga Click en Latest Python Release:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/928f0ff9-a94e-4284-9194-b071ebc83c8c

4.Baje hasta encontrarse con una tabla, a continuacion haga click en la version Recomendada:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ace290e-cdcc-48a9-8cae-f88dd4d44e53

5.Ejecute el archivo descargado, seleccione la opcion "Add python.exe to PATH", y haga click en "Customize Installation"

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16edf7c1-5820-44bd-a725-d8fa770800f1

6.Seleccione "Documentation", "pip", "tcl/tk and IDLE", "Python test Suite" y "py launcher", y haga click en "Next".

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2ec546af-350e-488d-b601-c769a426a8aa

7.Seleccione todas las opciones excepto "Install Python for all users", y NO MODIFIQUE "Customize Install location", haga click en "install":

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/631be840-ee4f-4212-b6c6-d6d82c46adf5

8.Espere hasta que se complete la installacion, y haga click en close:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/de4ec980-2a67-4b14-b451-e30560e99b49
